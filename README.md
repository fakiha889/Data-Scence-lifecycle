# Data-Scence-lifecycle
Data Science Lifecycle Projects
## Overview

This project demonstrates the six stages of the Data Science Lifecycle through two practical scenarios:

1. Telecom Customer Churn Analysis
2. Hospital Emergency Patient Waiting Time Analysis

The project covers goal setting, data retrieval, data preparation, data exploration, data modeling, and presentation and automation.

## Project 1: Customer Churn Analysis

### Description
This project focuses on predicting telecom customer churn to help businesses identify customers who may leave their services.

### Steps Performed
- **Step 1: Setting the Goal** – Define the research goal, business benefits, required data, timetable, and deliverables.
- **Step 2: Retrieving Data** – Create sample customer data containing monthly spending and customer tenure.
- **Step 3: Data Preparation** – Handle missing and negative values and integrate customer data with support-ticket information.
- **Step 4: Data Exploration (EDA)** – Generate descriptive statistics and analyze the relationship between spending and support tickets.
- **Step 5: Data Modeling** – Apply a basic rule-based function to predict customer churn risk.
- **Step 6: Presentation and Automation** – Generate alerts for customers identified as high churn risk.

## Project 2: Hospital Emergency Patient Waiting Time

### Description
This project analyzes hospital emergency patient waiting times to identify delays and help improve emergency department efficiency.

### Steps Performed
- **Step 1: Setting the Goal** – Define the research goal and identify the required patient data.
- **Step 2: Retrieving Data** – Create sample data containing patient age, emergency level, arrival time, waiting time, and department.
- **Step 3: Data Preparation** – Clean and prepare patient data for analysis.
- **Step 4: Data Exploration** – Generate descriptive statistics and examine the relationship between emergency level and waiting time.
- **Step 5: Data Modeling** – Apply basic rules to identify patients with potentially high waiting-time risk.
- **Step 6: Presentation and Automation** – Generate alerts for patients who may require a review of their waiting times.
